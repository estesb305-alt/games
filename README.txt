Rack-O app: how to put it online with Netlify

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop (log in to your Netlify account).
2. Drag this whole "rack-o-app" folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a link. Open it on a phone to play or install.

Installing on a phone
- Android (Chrome): tap "Install app" in the game, or Chrome's menu > Install app.
- iPhone/iPad (Safari): tap Share > Add to Home Screen.

Updating later
- Edit the files, change VERSION at the top of sw.js (for example rack-o-v2),
  then drag the folder onto your site's Deploys page in Netlify.
