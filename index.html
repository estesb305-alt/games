      How to play

      1. The goal. Get all 10 cards in your rack in order, lowest in slot 1 and highest in slot 10. The first player to do it calls Rack-O and ends the round.
      2. Your turn. Take the top card of the discard pile, or draw from the deck. A card from the discard pile has to go into your rack. A card from the deck can go in, or you can throw it away on the discard pile.
      3. Swapping. Tap a slot to put your card there. The card it replaces goes face up on the discard pile. You can never rearrange the cards in your rack.
      4. Scoring. You get 5 points for each card in order, counting from slot 1 until the order breaks. Calling Rack-O is worth 75 points. The first player to reach 500 wins.
      5. Bonus runs (optional). A run is 3 or more consecutive numbers in a row, like 24, 25, 26. When bonus runs are on, the player who goes Rack-O adds a bonus for their longest run: 50 for 3 cards, 100 for 4, 200 for 5, and 400 for 6 or more. When your rack is in order, you choose whether to call Rack-O now or keep building a longer run, at the risk of someone else going out first. Cards in a run have a gold underline.
      6. Tip. Put each card where its number belongs. With 40 cards, a card in the middle of that range belongs near the middle of your rack. The faint dots in your rack mark those spots, and the green slots are the ones already in order.
